# Instagram / TikTok Playbook

> Best for: products with visual output (Astraea's share cards are Reels-native). Short video is the highest-leverage free channel for consumer apps in 2026.

## Setup

- Business/creator account for analytics; bio link → landing page with `?ref=tiktok` / `?ref=ig`
- 9-grid preview: first 9 posts define the vibe — keep the midnight-blue/gold aesthetic consistent
- Cross-post every TikTok to Reels and Shorts (same file, native upload each — no watermarks)

## Content pillars (rotate)

1. **Readings on camera (3x/week):** screen-record a real tarot/compatibility reading with trending audio. Hook in first 1.5s: "The cards said WHAT about my love life?!"
2. **POV / relatable (2x/week):** "POV: the app says you're 94% compatible and now you're re-reading his texts" — text-overlay format, no filming needed
3. **Educational (1x/week):** "Your moon sign, not your sun sign, decides who you fall for — here's why" — positions authority
4. **UGC/duets (ongoing):** stitch bait — end videos with "duet with your result"; repost the best with credit

## Hooks that work in this niche

- Pattern interrupt in frame 1: a stunning share card filling the screen
- Open loops: "3 signs he's your twin flame according to your bazi — #2 surprised me"
- Comments bait (ethical): "Comment your moon sign and I'll reply with one line" — then actually reply; the algorithm rewards it

## Do / Don't

✅ Post native (film/edit in-app or upload clean files); ✅ reply to comments in the first hour;
✅ use 3–5 niche hashtags (#astrologytok #tarotreading #synastry), not 30;
❌ repost with another platform's watermark (TikTok logo on Reels = suppressed);
❌ "link in bio" in every caption — say it in 1 of 3 posts, pin a comment instead

## Cadence

- TikTok: 1/day for the first 30 days (volume finds winners), then 4–5/week keeping winners' format
- IG Reels: 4–5/week; Stories: daily polls ("pick a card: 🌙 or ⭐") for engagement
- Best times: 12–1pm and 7–10pm local time of your audience

## Metrics → `analytics/tracker.csv`

Views, avg watch time / completion rate, profile visits, bio-link clicks (`?ref=` split), followers. Kill any format with <15% completion after 5 tries.
