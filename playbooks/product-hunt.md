# Product Hunt Launch Playbook

> Best for: one-day traffic spikes + indie credibility. Pretty AI products overperform; dev tools need a technical hook.

## Timeline

**T-14 days:** pick your launch date — **Tuesday–Thursday** perform best; avoid launching against a huge TechCrunch-story product if you can. Line up a hunter (or self-launch; both work now).

**T-7 days:** finalize assets:
- Tagline ≤ 60 chars. Formula: [what] for [who], [differentiator]. Astraea example: "AI fortune-telling for love — bazi, tarot & astrology in one beautiful reading"
- Thumbnail: 240×240, must read at small size — your share card aesthetic is the asset
- Gallery: 4–6 screenshots/GIFs, first one = the money shot (a gorgeous compatibility card)
- Maker's first comment: your story in 150 words, why you built it, what's next. End with one question to prompt replies

**T-2 days:** message 30–50 people personally (not mass DM): "we launch Tuesday, would love your honest feedback". Warm list beats cold blast.

**Launch day (00:01 PT):**
- Post at midnight PT sharp; reply to **every** comment within 1 hour all day
- Ship a tiny feature mid-day ("by popular demand: rising-sign cards are live") — gives you an update post
- Coordinate socials: X thread, IG story, 小红书 note all saying "we're live 🔮" with the PH link
- War-room rule: one person owns replies, one owns socials, no one sleeps before 6pm PT

**T+1:** thank-you post + share results transparently (build-in-public gold). Email waitlist with the outcome.

## Do / Don't

✅ Ask for *feedback*, not upvotes ("would love your roast" converts better);
✅ be in the comments all day — makers who reply rank higher;
❌ vote manipulation / brigading — PH detects it and it backfires publicly;
❌ launching Friday–Sunday (dead zone) or the same week as Apple WWDC-scale events

## Metrics → `analytics/tracker.csv`

Upvotes, rank of day, website visits (`?ref=producthunt`), signups, comment count. A top-5 day typically = 2k–10k visits for a consumer product.
