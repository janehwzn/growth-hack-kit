# X / Twitter Playbook

> Best for: build-in-public, indie/SaaS launches, astro Twitter. Works alongside the pipeline's `x.txt` template (280-char aware).

## Setup

- Handle matches product name; bio = one-liner + landing link with `?ref=x`
- Pinned post: the "why I built this" story (not a feature list)
- Turn on analytics; check weekly, not daily

## Tactics (in order)

1. **Build-in-public thread (weekly):** one concrete thing you shipped + a screenshot. Formula: problem → weird detail → result. Example hook: "I built an AI fortune-teller with a zero-token astrology engine. Here's why the LLM never touches the math 🧵"
2. **Daily mini-readings (for Astraea):** tie transits to relatable advice. "Venus in Scorpio today. Don't text him. Text your group chat instead." — quote-postable, native to astro Twitter.
3. **Reply-guy strategy (20 min/day):** thoughtful replies on 5–10 larger accounts in your niche *before* you need them. No links, just value. This is how small accounts get discovered.
4. **Share-card drops:** post actual user-style outputs ("POV: the app says you're 94% compatible"). Let people quote-post their own results — that's the loop.
5. **Launch/hype posts:** only after 2–4 weeks of value. "We're live on Product Hunt 🔮" with one screenshot and one ask.

## Example hooks

- "Your moon sign explains your 2am decisions better than your therapist"
- "I combined Chinese bazi (with true solar time correction) + western astrology in one app. Astrologers, come fight me"
- "Day 12 of building an AI astrology app nobody asked for: the share cards finally look expensive"

## Do / Don't

✅ One idea per post; ✅ native formatting (line breaks, no hashtag stuffing — 1–2 max);
❌ link-in-every-post (kills reach — put the link in a reply to your own post);
❌ engagement pods / follow-for-follow; ❌ auto-DM spam

## Cadence

- 1–3 posts/day + 5–10 genuine replies; best times 8–10am and 7–9pm PT (12–2pm ET for EU overlap is fine too)
- Threads: 1/week max. Quality bar: would *you* read all 8 tweets?

## Metrics → `analytics/tracker.csv`

Profile visits, follows, link clicks (`?ref=x`), top-post impressions. Weekly retro: which hook format won? Double down, kill the rest.
